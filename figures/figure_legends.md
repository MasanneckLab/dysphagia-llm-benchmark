# Revised figure legends

Figure 1. Final consensus rating distributions. Blue, yellow and red represent Correct, Inaccurate/Incomplete and Wrong, respectively. Each system contributes 90 responses.

Figure 2. Descriptive paired differences in 0-2 ratings. Positive differences indicate a higher mean coded rating for the first system; the scale does not imply equally spaced clinical benefit. Error bars are percentile 95% intervals from 20,000 domain-stratified topic-pair bootstrap resamples, retaining both formats and all systems. No multiplicity-adjusted inference is attached to these descriptive intervals.

Figure 3. Final consensus ratings by clinical domain. Each bar summarizes 30 responses per system per domain; colors match Figure 1. The pooled domain pattern is descriptive and does not identify a causal domain-difficulty mechanism.

Figure 4. Source support among final consensus-Correct responses. The endpoint-specific Yes counts and model-specific Correct denominators are printed above bars. Panel A shows guideline support; panel B shows guideline OR high-level support. CH recorded the final source-support consensus with BL. Error bars are domain-stratified topic-pair bootstrap 95% intervals. There are no between-system significance annotations. Sonar-RP denotes Sonar-Reasoning-Pro.

Figure 5. Knowledge versus case formats. Panel A shows Correct proportions among 45 responses per format per system, with domain-stratified topic-pair bootstrap 95% intervals. Panel B shows descriptive knowledge-minus-case differences on the 0-2 scale, with paired topic-pair bootstrap intervals. Lack of detected differences does not demonstrate equivalence or broad clinical reasoning.

Additional figure. Guideline concordance and the joint Correct AND guideline-supported endpoint use a common denominator of 90 per system. Error bars are topic-pair bootstrap intervals. The joint endpoint is not a measurement of citation quality in noncorrect answers. In all figures, Sonar-RP denotes Sonar-Reasoning-Pro; CH denotes Cinja Huber and BL denotes Bendix Labeit.
